# HIIKKY - Project Introduction

## Overview

HIIKKY started as a Subscription & Billing Management System and evolved into a smarter and more scalable platform for subscription lifecycle management.

The project focuses on solving real-world business challenges by bringing subscription and billing-related activities into a centralized system.

## Key Areas

HIIKKY focuses on:

- Subscription Management
- Smart Billing and Invoicing
- Auto Renewals and Payment Tracking
- Revenue and Subscription Analytics
- Discount and Coupon Management
- Course Subscription Mapping
- Smart Notifications

## Product Design

Before implementation, AI-assisted design was used to visualize the complete product and make the backend development process more structured.

Google Stitch AI was explored to design the UI/UX of HIIKKY and understand how the complete product could be organized before development.

The goal was to create a modern and user-friendly platform for both businesses and customers.

## Project Direction

HIIKKY is being developed step by step, with each module contributing to the overall subscription lifecycle.

The project is focused on building the backend and gradually developing HIIKKY into an industry-ready subscription and billing management solution.
